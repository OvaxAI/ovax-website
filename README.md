# ovax-website
OVAX-AI ·

OVAX AI is an AI-powered legal knowledge platform built for the digital asset ecosystem.

Our mission is to make legal information easier to access, understand, and navigate by combining artificial intelligence with a growing global legal knowledge base.

Instead of searching through thousands of pages of regulations, users can simply ask questions and receive structured, easy-to-understand legal information related to digital assets.

OVAX AI is designed to become a bridge between technology and legal professionals, helping users make more informed decisions in an increasingly complex digital world.

## Technology Repositories

- OVAX Website — public interface
- OVAX Legal Engine — legal document indexing and analysis
- OVAX Smart Contracts — verified BEP-20 contracts
